### **Mobile Engineering & Android Architecture**

I am currently working at **[Tiket.com](https://tiket.com)** as a **Senior Software Engineer** with over **3 years of hands-on experience** building scalable, high-performance applications. My journey in tech began with a passion for solving complex problems, which naturally led me to explore the world of mobile technology — particularly native Android, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Firebase. Over time, this curiosity evolved into a deep commitment to building not just apps, but also **knowledge-sharing communities.**

Prior to joining Tiket.com, I worked at Quinbay (focusing on the [blibli.com](http://blibli.com) ecosystem), where I contributed to critical mobile infrastructure and Android experiences that serve millions of users within a robust omnichannel commerce platform. My work included developing native features, integrating Firebase Cloud services, architecting offline-capable workflows, and optimizing app performance and reliability in production-level environments.
